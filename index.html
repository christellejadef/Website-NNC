About BSCS

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) prepares students for careers in the rapidly changing world of technology. Students develop skills in programming, software development, databases, web development, algorithms, and computer systems.

What skills you can develop by taking BSCS, you can develop skills in:

  • Coding and programming
  • Database management
  • Logical and analytical thinking
  • Web and application development
